POLARIN Training Hub

A collection of training resources for polar research.

Training resources

Fieldwork planning Safety Logistics, permits and regulations Ethical and equitable collaboration Reducing environmental impact Scientific instrumentation and data acquisition Data management and analysis Science communication

POLARIN is an international network of polar research infrastructures and services designed to address the scientific challenges of the polar regions.

The Association of Polar Early Career Scientists (APECS) is an international and transdisciplinary organization for early career professionals with interests in polar regions and the wider cryosphere.

The POLARIN Training Hub is under actively development and includes experimental features and content.
