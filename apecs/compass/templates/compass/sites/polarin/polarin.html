POLARIN training resources

Planning a safe, sustainable and efficient stay at a polar research infrastructure

Research activities in polar regions require specialised knowledge and preparation. Accessing and working at polar research infrastructures often involves complex logistics, challenging environmental conditions, strict regulatory frameworks, and the need to minimise environmental impacts on vulnerable ecosystems. The material in the POLARIN Training Hub is centered around the following key areas:

Planning and conducting safe polar fieldwork

Keep people and the environment safe during your fieldwork activities in the polar regions through planning and the implementation of safety protocols.

Collaborating meaningfully

Collaborate with other researchers, Indigenous Peoples, and local communities equitably and ethically to advance your research.

Reducing environmental impact

Reduce your research's environmental impact by using sustainable and responsible practices.

Using research infrastructures efficiently

Use polar research infrastructures efficiently and manage your data to achieve your research goals and increase the societal benefit of your work.

The resources in the POLARIN Training Hub provide an introduction to planning and conducting safe, sustainable and efficient research activities at polar research infrastructures but do not replace guidelines and training from your local research institute or infrastructure.

Remember to seek guidance and direct training from your local research institute or infrastructure to ensure that your research is safe and effective!

Polar Research Infrastructure Network

About POLARIN

POLARIN is an international network of polar research infrastructures (RIs) and services designed to address the scientific challenges of the polar regions. This network encompasses a diverse range of RIs, including Arctic and Antarctic research stations, research vessels, icebreakers operating at both poles, observatories, data infrastructures, and repositories for ice and sediment cores. POLARIN aims to provide integrated, challenge-driven access to these infrastructures, facilitating interdisciplinary research on complex polar processes.

Association of Polar Early Career Scientists

About APECS

The Association of Polar Early Career Scientists (APECS) is an international and transdisciplinary organization for early career professionals with interests in polar regions and the wider cryosphere. APECS aims to foster an international network of polar early career community members across disciplines and professions to facilitate multi-directional knowledge exchange and capacity building. It facilitates career development, provides support through training and resources, and serves as a platform that empowers and advocates for the polar early career community.
